# Jin: technical artist review

**1. Who I am, what I looked at.** Jin, technical artist: I turn art-direction wishes into 60 fps on a 2019 integrated-GPU laptop. I studied all 14 baseline shots and read `renderer.ts`, `antdraw.ts`, `backdrop.ts`, `palette.ts`, `camera.ts`, `plants.ts` and `director.ts`. I took 32 shots (big fixture) and built a **runtime macro-LOD prototype**, injected into my own headless page by wrapping `renderer.painter.update`; no repo file touched (`macro_proto.js`, `ONESHOT_*.png`). Timings come from SwiftShader on a shared CPU: JS numbers are real, GPU numbers are my estimates.

**2. Style change on zoom: partly yes.** The 1 mm cell stays the data and colour truth at every zoom. Only geometry reconstruction changes, once a cell is far wider than an ant's leg. Below z5, nothing changes. At z5, where a chamfer or stroked stem differs from the squares by 1–2 px, switch representation. Then morph parameters continuously to full at z8: contour smoothing, wall blur, texture strength, stroke taper. The director's subject shots (z8–11) land fully inside. Never alpha-crossfade two geometries: at z6.5 stems and roots ghosted double (`p_granary_z6.5_after.png`).

**3. Findings** (ordered by payoff per engineering day)

1. **[P1] Nurseries read as flooded.** Their teal back wall (`z3_mid`, `z4_entrance`, `b01`) is close to water in hue. → `ZONE_TINT.nursery` to warm grey, or mix 0.38→0.15. 0.1 day, free.
2. **[P1] Ants vanish on the back wall at macro.** #261a12 bodies sit on a wall mixed 50% toward #120b07 (shaft in `b01`, night `b06`). → At s≥8, lift `backwall()` about 12% and raise the rim alpha in `AntPainter.ant()` from 0.3 to 0.5. 0.25 day, free.
3. **[P1] 106 ms hitch whenever zoom crosses 5** (measured full `updateBase()`). `render()` sets `w.dirtyAll` when `closeUp` flips. Every cut into or out of the director's wide shot (about 1.7× fit, roughly z2) crosses 5. That puts a stutter in the shareable clips; `zonesChanged` does the same. → Re-colour only SEED/REFUSE cells (or only zoned cells). 0.5 day, under 1 ms.
4. **[P0] Plants are the loudest pixels at macro.** Their 14 px staircase stems sit against smooth bokeh, beside vector ants (`z7`, `macro.jpg`). `Plants.design()` rounds curves to cells, but `Plant.parts` keeps growth order. → At z≥5, drop plant cells in `cellColor()` and stroke the part chains (3 smoothing passes, taper, thin highlight). Draw phase incl. plants is 1.2 ms. 1.5 days, including a real clover trefoil (my discs look bubbly).
5. **[P1] Soil reads as flat tiles.** → Damp the tone mosaic 50% toward the 3×3 mean, plus one world-anchored crumb/sand tile (16 texels/mm) as an `overlay` pattern, alpha ramped z5→8. One full-screen fill, est. 0.5–1 ms GPU. 1 day; per-horizon tiles later.
6. **[P1] Lone cells become 14 px squares.** Charcoal flecks, the white "brick" stone (`b01`), and dark ×0.72 squares between seed ovals inside heaps (`c03_pop_4.95_vs_5.00.png`). → Flecks as hashed ellipses (like `grains()`), stones as shaded contour fills, heap interiors into the soft wall. About 150 shapes, under 0.3 ms. 1 day.
7. **[P0] Staircase tunnels and chambers are the biggest tell** (`z5`, `z6`, `granary.jpg`). → Add `macroSoil()` after `drawImage(this.wc)`: marching squares over the visible rect, with crossings on a 3×3-smoothed field but binary topology, so 1-cell tunnels survive. `clip()` to it, draw the back wall bilinear (another focal plane, behind the glass), stroke a contact shadow, and redraw the backdrop through the smoothed ground line. The prototype adds 3–13 ms at z14, 68% of it marching squares, unoptimised and rebuilt every frame. Cached per 32×32 tile and invalidated by `w.dirtyList`, I estimate under 1 ms CPU plus 1–2 ms GPU. 4 days. Risk: walls shift up to 0.3 mm, so feet can float about 4 px.
8. **[P2] Last pixel tells.** Roots stay blocky (`f_follow_z11_after`), and bilinear only smears them (`q_t_z9`). → Store root polylines in `Plants.roots()` (save-format change, 1.5 days). Camera easing also leaves fractional zooms where cells come out 3 or 4 px wide and crawl. → Prescale by floor(z) nearest, then bilinear the rest (one blit, 0.5 day).

**4. The one screenshot.** `ONESHOT_follow_z11.png`: big fixture, noon, zoom 11, following a forager at the entrance. Before, the mound is stepped, the grass is pixels and the shaft is square. After, palette, lighting and ants are unchanged. The mound and ground line are smooth, crumb-textured slopes. The shaft and nursery have rounded walls, a 0.7 mm contact shadow and a defocused back wall. Grass becomes tapered curves with arching blades and seed ovals, flecks become grains, and the pebble is a shaded oval. The ants finally belong to the photograph. A granary version is in `ONESHOT_granary_z14.png`.

**5. What not to touch.**
- The ant painter's LOD by on-screen size and its cost discipline: static Path2D per caste, legs drawn as one stroke.
- The cheap, half-resolution out-of-focus backdrop.
- The cell grid below z5. The horizons and roots in `hero.jpg` make the best whole-slice frame.
