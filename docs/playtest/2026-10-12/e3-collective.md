# e3 collective behaviour
Method: seeds 42, 7, 1234, 7-10 days; tracked 15 ants every 80 min; 39 controlled forks from a day-2.5 save, one change, +6 days. Deterministic; fork 37 ticks later ±0.5%; seeds ±10%. Read GDD + colony/ant/nest.ts.
Verdict: looks alive (polyethism, trunk-trail column, shelters from rain, digs harder after). As colony brain most choices absorbed by self-correction; exceptions are switches that kill the colony without a word.
Findings: [P0] Forage Off kills in 4 days (46 ants kept digging while starving; toast blamed foragers); Nurse Off: 124 larvae starved next to 110-210 stored seeds, never logged -> Off as soft limit hunger can override; log brood starvation with cause. [P1] most settings no difference (Forage Low ≈ Normal; majors inconsistent; Clean Off 664 husks + 7 corpses, no consequence). [P1] almost no granaries: at 200-300 workers 9-14 rest, 1 granary, 1 nursery; seed-42 forks 1-20 seeds stored -> weight planner to stores, stores as weather buffer. [P1] majors cost, no benefit (code: dig 25% faster, slower, +2 larval feedings; journal says seed-cracking jaws). [P1] brood barely visible: 14 brood / 270 workers; development 0.24 days vs life 3-5 days -> lengthen development. [P2] shaft single-file line of overlapping ants -> second entrance/lanes (Dussutour 2004). [P2] rain: foragers 32->11 outside but 25-35 diggers kept dumping outside. [P2] journal spam: 6 sprouting lines same timestamp.
Believable: nurse->dig->forage by age; 10-30% idle reserve; foragers rush home in rain. Breaks: obeys orders that kill it; single scent band; chambers placed by central planner (top-down).
Levers (day 8.8 vs baseline 204/234/235): Forage Off/Nurse Off dead; Forage Low/High -1/+17, -11/+1, +3/+17; Dig Off -8% (area -78%), -28%, -25%; Dig High -6, -5; Breeding High -14, +39, +17; Breeding Low -21, -38, -23 (595 seeds stored); Majors 35/0 -10/+2, +12/+3, +17/+9; Clean Off +1, 4 granaries +1, 2 nurseries -14; marker x=120 -1%, new chambers x 162-271 instead of 297-374. Only Breeding, Dig Off, marker are real decisions. Breeding High with little food never >9 starvation deaths.
Mechanics: response thresholds + crisis override (Bonabeau, Theraulaz & Deneubourg 1996; Theraulaz 1998) M; return-rate regulated foraging (Gordon 2013; Prabhakar 2012) M; patchy seed + trail lifetime (Deneubourg 1990; Beckers 1992; Beekman 2001) S-M; crowding-driven digging, marker as scent cue (Rasse & Deneubourg 2001; Buhl 2004 Messor sancta; Khuong 2016) M-L; vertical zoning (Tschinkel 2004; Tschinkel & Hanley 2017) M; majors as millers (Heredia & Detrain 2005) M; hygiene & disease (Hart & Ratnieks 2001; Cremer 2007) M; quorum emigration when flooded (Pratt 2002; Franks 2003) L.
